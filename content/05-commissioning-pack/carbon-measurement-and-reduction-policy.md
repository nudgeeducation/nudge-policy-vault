# Carbon Measurement and Reduction Policy

> **Operated by:** Nudge Education Ltd · **Version:** Jan 2025 · **Owner:** Director of Operations

Nudge Education Carbon Measurement and Reduction Policy

JAN 2025
Review Date: DEC 2025

Introduction Nudge Education is committed to making a positive
environmental impact by reducing its carbon footprint while delivering
critical interventions for chronically disengaged children. As our operations
involve extensive travel across the United Kingdom, we recognise the
importance of balancing our essential activities with responsible
environmental stewardship. This policy outlines our approach to
measuring, reducing, and offsetting carbon emissions, contributing to a
sustainable future.

Goals and Commitments Our carbon measurement and reduction
strategy aims to:

## 1. Measure: Accurately assess our current carbon emissions, focusing
on travel and transportation.
## 2. Reduce: Implement strategies to minimise emissions without
compromising the quality of our service.
## 3. Offset: Where reductions are not feasible, offset emissions through
credible programs.
## 4. Educate and Engage: Foster a culture of environmental awareness
within our organization.

Measuring Our Carbon Footprint We will:

- Conduct an annual audit to quantify emissions, with a primary focus
on vehicle use.
- Use recognised tools and methodologies, such as the Greenhouse
Gas Protocol or online carbon calculators, to ensure accuracy and
reliability.
- Maintain records of fuel consumption, mileage, and any other
relevant data.

Reduction Strategies To minimise our environmental impact, we commit
to:

## 1. Optimising Travel:
- Encourage regional assignments to reduce long-distance
travel.
- Promote virtual meetings and online collaboration where
feasible to minimise unnecessary trips.
- Develop efficient travel routes for practitioners using route
planning tools.
## 2. Vehicle Use:
- Gradually transition to low-emission or electric vehicles,
prioritising procurement of such vehicles for organisational
use.
- Implement carpooling policies when multiple practitioners are
traveling to similar areas.
## 3. Operational Adjustments:
- Incentivise the use of public transportation where practical
and safe.
- Invest in technology and tools that reduce the need for
physical travel, such as virtual tutoring platforms.

Offsetting Emissions For emissions that cannot be avoided, we will:

- Partner with reputable carbon offset programs focusing on
reforestation, renewable energy projects, or community initiatives.
- Ensure transparency in our offsetting efforts by publishing annual
reports.

Monitoring and Reporting We will:

- Set a baseline year for carbon emissions and track progress annually
against reduction targets.
- Establish a reduction target of 20% in vehicle emissions over five
years, adjusting targets as needed based on performance and
feasibility.
- Publish an annual Environmental Impact Report to share progress
and challenges with stakeholders.

Education and Engagement We believe in empowering our team and
stakeholders to make sustainable choices. To this end, we will:

- Provide training and resources for practitioners to understand and
contribute to our sustainability goals.
- Encourage feedback and suggestions from our team to improve our
environmental strategies.
- Partner with schools, local communities, and environmental
organizations to amplify impact.

Review and Continuous Improvement This policy will be reviewed
annually to reflect advancements in technology, evolving best practices,
and feedback from stakeholders. We remain committed to setting and
achieving realistic yet ambitious targets that align with our mission and
environmental responsibilities.

Conclusion Nudge Education is dedicated to fostering a culture of
care—for our young people, our communities, and our planet. By
embedding sustainability into our operations, we aim to set a positive
example and contribute to a healthier, more sustainable future for all.

This policy is effective as of 20.1.25 and will be reviewed on an annual basis.

REVIEW & REVISION

This policy will be reviewed annually or as required to ensure its
effectiveness and alignment with best practices within Nudge.
