## About this pack

Nudge Education Ltd holds professional indemnity, public liability, and employer's liability insurance appropriate to the provision delivered, including online delivery via Nudge Education Online (NEO). The full pack of current certificates is held in the commissioning documentation folder and provided to commissioners on request as part of the procurement process.
