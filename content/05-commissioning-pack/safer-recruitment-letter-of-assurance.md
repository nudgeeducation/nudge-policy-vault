# Safer Recruitment Letter of Assurance

> **Operated by:** Nudge Education Ltd · **Version:** Oct 2024 · **Owner:** Director of Operations

Nudge Education,

2 Sirius House,
Amethyst Road,
Newcastle upon Tyne,
NE4 7YL

Wednesday October 2024

To whom it may concern,

Safeguarding and Safer Recruitment: Letter of Assurance

In accordance with the requirements of the DfE guidance 'Keeping Children Safe in
Education', I confirm that Nudge Education has undertaken all the necessary
safeguarding, safer recruitment and vetting and barring checks for all its employees.

This includes:
- Enhanced DBS check
- Verification of identity
- Right to work in the United Kingdom
- Overseas checks for members of staff who have been abroad for more than three
months during the last five years
- Provision of references and a full employment history.

Yours faithfully,

Claire Falcus
Operations Manager
