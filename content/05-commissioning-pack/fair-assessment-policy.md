# Fair Assessment Policy

> **Operated by:** Nudge Education Ltd · **Version:** Dec 2025 · **Owner:** Director of Operations

FAIR ASSESSMENT POLICY
DEC 2025
Review date: DEC 2026

SCOPE
Nudge Education delivers bespoke interventions to support young people
academically, socially and emotionally, helping them progress toward their next
steps; whether that is reintegration into school, transition to a new provision, or
entering the world of work.

This policy ensures that all young people that are supported by Nudge Education
have the same opportunities to access appropriate qualifications, should that be
the focus of their intervention, and that no young person is disadvantaged based
on any attribute or circumstance (e.g. language, disability).

STATEMENT OF INTENT
Nudge Education aims to provide all young people accessing our service the best
chance of success when undertaking qualifications and examinations.

Where interventions follow Nudge Education's Academic Pathway, we will
gradually progress towards more formal academic settings and always facilitate a
safe and productive environment, suitable for study. We will provide academic
support in sessions should this be the focus of the intervention, aligned with the
National Curriculum and specific exam specifications where necessary.

In line with our Equality and Diversity policy, this document relates to the fair
assessment of a student's work, reasonable adjustments that may need to be
made and links to our Complaints Policy should there any formal concerns.

We also aim to write our policies and guides in language that is concise and easy
to understand.

Nudge Education is not a registered exam centre and so we work closely with our
commissioners and local partners to access exam centres, should exams or formal
qualifications be requested for a young person accessing support from Nudge.
Exam or qualification content is embedded into the intervention with a suitably
trained/qualified Practitioner matched to the intervention.

WHAT YOUNG PEOPLE CAN EXPECT
- We will prepare young people to access exams and qualifications that are
appropriate and achievable for their level of learning. We want to ensure that
young people are set up to achieve, not fail.

- In some cases, we will notify our Commissioners when we believe a young
person is ready to undertake an exam or qualification and can adapt our
interventions to incorporate any learning accordingly.

- We will use diagnostic assessment tools, such as Functional Skills and GCSE
modules on BKSB.

- We aim to ensure that all assessment of work is carried out fairly and in
keeping with the Awarding Body's requirements.

- Timely and fair feedback on a young person's work will be given throughout
the duration of their studies or qualification.

- We will work with each young person to ensure that their learning style and
preferences are met by their allocated Practitioner.

- We will not discriminate against any young person or carry out teaching or
assessment in a manner that is likely to disadvantage a certain group of
people or give an advantage to a certain group of people, for example giving
out learning and assessment resources that are not written in the young
person's first language.

- We will work closely with our commissioners to ensure that young people are
entered into exams or qualifications in a timely manner and with the support
required for them to be undertaken.

- Where appropriate or necessary, Nudge Practitioners can be involved in a
regular standardisation process to ensure that marking, assessment and
delivery of qualifications is of a similar standard for all young people.

- Education Intervention Coordinators (EICs) will request from commissioners or
other partners that applications are made where any reasonable adjustments
(or Access Arrangements) need to be made.

All young people who are to be enrolled on a formal accredited qualification will
be given the following information:

- The named Practitioner(s) likely to deliver and support the young person
throughout the qualification.

- Content of the course including expected learning hours, assessment
methods and level of qualification.

- Deadlines for coursework submission and examination dates.

- Information, advice and guidance around the potential progression routes
for the student following the successful completion of the named
qualification.

- Which awarding body the qualification is accredited by.

- What happens if the young person fails an exam, project or module.

- What resources will be available to the young person to undertake their
qualification, including any reasonable adjustments that need to be made.

- Rules and boundaries relating to the limits of support that Nudge
Education can provide and consequences of malpractice or cheating.

INFORMATION SHARING & DATA PROTECTION

As part of legislation and guidelines relating to data protection and security such
as the Data Protection Act 1998 and the General Data Protection Regulations (May
2018). Nudge Education will use personal information to register students for
accredited qualifications. We follow legal protocols and enhanced security
procedures to keep confidential information as safe as possible and we assess the
policies and procedures of external organisations such as Awarding Bodies to
limit any leakage of this data.

Further information can be found in the Nudge Education Data Protection and
GDPR Policy.

ACCESSIBILITY OF POLICY
This policy will be openly available to young people, parents (including Corporate
Parents for Looked After Children), carers, commissioners, all staff at Nudge
Education as well as any representatives from Awarding Bodies (e.g. ASDAN,
NCFE, City and Guilds) and Regulatory Bodies (such as OFSTED or ISI).

Copies of this policy can be sent in electronic and printed formats upon request
by commissioners and interested parties.

REVIEW
