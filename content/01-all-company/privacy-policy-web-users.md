What we collect

How we get it

Why we use it & lawful bases
- Respond to your enquiries; secure, operate and improve the site — Legitimate
interests (Article 6(1)(f))

set these until you consent via the banner (PECR).

Cookies
- Strictly necessary cookies run without consent.
- All other cookies (analytics/marketing/social) run only with your consent via our
cookie banner. You can change choices anytime via "Cookie Settings".

Sharing

they act on our instructions under Article 28 agreements

International transfers
Some providers/sub-processors may be outside the UK. We use UK adequacy decisions
and/or the UK IDTA/UK Addendum to SCCs plus supplementary measures where
needed.

How long we keep it
- Website enquiries/CRM contacts: up to 7 years or until you ask us to remove you.

any time.

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, plus the right to

Cookie Policy
What are cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the
site function, keep it secure, remember your choices, and (with your permission) help us
understand how people use our site.

We also use similar technologies (e.g., pixels, local storage, SDKs). In this policy, "cookies"
includes these tools.

Personal data: Analytics can capture IP addresses and online identifiers
which are personal data under UK GDPR.

Our legal basis and consent (PECR + UK GDPR)
- Strictly necessary cookies run on the basis of our legitimate interests (Article
6(1)(f)) and do not require consent under PECR.

- All other cookies (analytics, advertising, social media) are set only with your
consent (Article 6(1)(a)). We use a cookie banner/manager to collect and record
your choices and to block non-essential cookies until you opt in,

You can change or withdraw consent at any time.

How we use cookies (categories)
- Strictly necessary – required for core site functions (security, load balancing, login,
cookie consent records).
Preferences – remember settings (e.g., language).
Analytics – measure usage so we can improve our content and services. We
configure analytics to minimise data and enable IP masking where possible.
- Marketing/social – measure campaign performance or enable social media
embeds. We only use these with your consent.

Third-party cookies
Some cookies are set by third parties (e.g., Google, Meta, LinkedIn) when their content or
tags load on our pages. These third parties control their cookies and may use them for
their own purposes. See their privacy/cookie notices for details. We only load them after
you consent.

International transfers
Some providers (or their sub-processors) may process data outside the UK (e.g., the US).
Where that happens, we rely on UK adequacy regulations and/or the UK International
Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or UK Addendum to the EU SCCs, plus additional
measures where needed. Ask us for a copy or summary of the relevant safeguards.

How to change or withdraw your consent
- Use the "Cookie Settings" link in our footer to review or change your choices at
any time.
- You can also clear cookies via your browser; this removes existing cookies but may
not prevent new ones unless you change your browser settings.

Managing cookies in your browser
You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Guidance is available from providers like:

- Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari (see in-browser help), or
- independent guides such as allaboutcookies.org (external resource).

Blocking some cookies may impact site functionality.

Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control
If your browser sends Global Privacy Control (GPC) or Do Not Track signals, we treat
them as an opt-out from non-essential cookies where technically feasible, but please also
use Cookie Settings for the most reliable control.

How long we keep cookie data
- Analytics data are retained only as long as necessary for reporting and
improvement; we regularly review retention settings.
- Where possible, we aggregate or anonymise analytics to reduce personal data use.

Updates to this policy
We'll update this policy if our cookies or providers change, or if law/guidance changes.
We'll post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date. Your current privacy
materials already commit to posting updates and, where appropriate, notifying by email.
