Slack Usage Policy
December 2025
Review date: December 2026

Applies To: All practitioners and staff engaged with Nudge Education

Nudge Education – Usage of Slack

Our Slack workspace is a collaborative and inclusive platform designed to
facilitate communication and teamwork.

To ensure a positive and respectful environment, we ask all members to
adhere to the following policy:

Respect and Inclusivity

We are proud of the diverse backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives
that make up our team. In all Slack interactions, we expect every member
to treat others with courtesy, kindness, and respect. Language and tone
should be inclusive and considerate, free from any offensive or
discriminatory remarks. We aim to create an environment where every
voice is valued and all individuals feel safe and heard.

Professionalism

Slack is a professional space, and the way we communicate here should
reflect that. Personal attacks, inappropriate language, or any form of
harassment are not acceptable. Additionally, we ask that team members
refrain from sharing confidential or sensitive information in public
channels. Private channels or direct messages should be used for any
communication that requires discretion.

Collaboration and Communication

Slack is a key tool for supporting collaboration across the organisation. We
encourage open dialogue and knowledge-sharing that helps us all learn
and grow. To help keep discussions accessible and easy to navigate, please
use the most appropriate channels for each topic. Organised
communication makes it easier for everyone to stay informed and
contribute meaningfully.

Minimising Disruptions

To maintain a focused and productive workspace, avoid spamming
channels with repetitive or irrelevant content. Likewise, direct messages
and mentions should be used thoughtfully and never for unsolicited or
disruptive communication.

Using Channels Thoughtfully

We ask that channels be used according to their intended purpose. This
helps keep conversations relevant and well-organised. Before creating a
new channel, consider whether an existing one could serve the same
purpose — keeping things streamlined helps everyone.

Time Zone Awareness

With colleagues potentially working across various time zones, it's
important to be considerate when posting messages or scheduling
discussions. Where possible, use Slack's scheduling features to avoid
sending messages outside of someone's working hours.

Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that goes against this Code of
Conduct, we ask that you report it to your Line Manager promptly. In
situations where the Line Manager may be involved in the concern, please
reach out directly to Human Resources. All reports will be treated seriously
and handled with care, ensuring that appropriate steps are taken following
a fair investigation.

Alignment with Wider Policies

This Code of Conduct is part of our broader commitment to a positive
working culture. All expectations outlined here should be followed
alongside our wider organisational policies and guidance.

By using Slack at Nudge, you agree to uphold the standards set out in this
Code of Conduct. Breaches may lead to disciplinary action, including
removal of Slack access where necessary.

We appreciate your commitment to making Slack a space where
collaboration, respect, and professionalism flourish.
