CCTV Usage and Data Management Policy

December 2025
Review Date: December 2026

Introduction

At Nudge Education, we highly value the security and safety of our
premises and staff. To support this, we employ Closed-Circuit Television
(CCTV) systems as essential tools in creating a secure and welcoming work
environment. This policy outlines our responsible approach to CCTV usage,
ensuring compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR),
UK laws, and our broader data security and privacy commitments.

Policy Scope

This policy applies to all CCTV systems and related recordings used within
and around our office locations. It encompasses all areas monitored by
cameras to observe and record activities.

Objectives

We use CCTV for the following core purposes:
1. To safeguard our physical assets and office spaces.
2. To enhance the safety and well-being of all employees and visitors.
## 3. To deter and prevent unauthorised activities such as theft and
vandalism.
## 4. To assist in investigating accidents and incidents by providing reliable
evidence when needed.

Compliance with Legislation

Our CCTV practices are in full alignment with GDPR and other pertinent
legislation. This ensures our surveillance is justifiable, proportionate, and
respectful of everyone's privacy. We make sure our practices are
transparent and carried out with fairness.

Data Collection and Fair Use

Transparency: We clearly display signs where CCTV is operational, providing
transparency about surveillance and its intent.
Purpose Limitation: We do not use CCTV for monitoring staff performance
or for any purpose not clearly specified in this policy without explicit
justification and communication.

Data Storage and Access

Standard Retention Period: Normally, CCTV footage is kept for 21 days as
facilitated by our Hik-Connect system. This duration allows ample time for
reviewing footage following any incidents.
Extended Retention Conditions: In cases involving potential legal or HR
issues, such as accidents or disciplinary matters, relevant footage may be
retained as part of our HR records for up to 7 years.
Access Control: Access to CCTV footage is strictly limited to authorised
personnel, ensuring that privacy and data protection principles are upheld.

Rights of Individuals

Individuals have rights under the GDPR, including the right to access
footage in which they are identifiable. We handle access requests promptly
and respectfully, in line with legal requirements.

Data Security and Integrity
Protection Measures: We employ robust security measures to protect CCTV
footage against unauthorised access or misuse. This includes secure
storage and, where necessary, encryption.
Data Accuracy: We conduct regular checks and maintenance to ensure
that our CCTV systems function correctly and record accurately.

Data Disposal

Once the standard retention period has elapsed, or after extended
retention periods end, CCTV footage is securely deleted. We use methods
such as data overwriting or physical destruction of storage media to
prevent any possibility of data recovery.

Review and Monitoring

We regularly review this policy to ensure it remains up-to-date with
technological, legal, and operational changes. We also welcome feedback
from our staff and stakeholders to enhance the effectiveness and
sensitivity of our surveillance practices.

By adhering to this policy, Nudge Education is committed to using CCTV in
a manner that is both responsible and considerate, balancing security
needs with the privacy rights of individuals. We remain dedicated to
continuous evaluation and improvement of our surveillance procedures.

Should you have any questions or concerns about our CCTV policy or
practices, please do not hesitate to contact our Data Protection Officer at
brian.mair@nudgeeducation.co.uk
